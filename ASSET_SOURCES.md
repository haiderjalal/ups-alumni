# Asset sources

Every image, logo, font and third-party file used by the site is recorded here, with its source,
its permission state, and how it is used. Update this file whenever an asset changes.

| Asset | Source | Permission state | Used for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Wordmark "UPS Alumni Network" | Typeset in the site's display font. Not an image. | Original work of the project | Header, footer, sign-in | Not an official school crest. |
| Placeholder artwork (`PhotoPlaceholder`) | Drawn in code (SVG paths inside the component). | Original work of the project | Homepage hero, about page | Nonrepresentational. It does not show any real place or person. |
| University of Malakand logo (`https://www.uom.edu.pk/assets/frontend/images/UOMlogo.png`) | Official site of the university | **Not used.** Identified only as a candidate; not verified as a UPS asset. | None | Do not use as the UPS crest. Do not hotlink. |

## Approved school assets

None yet. When the school supplies an approved logo or photograph, record it here with:

- The source (who supplied it and when).
- The permission state (written approval; for people, written consent from each identifiable person).
- The alt text.
- Where it is used.

## Rules

- No photographs of identifiable people are published without written consent.
- Do not label unrelated stock imagery as UPS.
- Do not hotlink images from third-party sites or logo aggregators.
- Do not generate or redraw an official emblem.
